# Contributing

1. Fork, then branch from `main`.
2. Build and try it: `./scripts/install.sh`.
3. Keep pull requests small and focused, with a short note on what changed and how you tested it.

Commit messages: one sentence, capital first letter, no period, no `feat:` prefixes (e.g. `Fix timer not stopping after sleep`).

Bugs: include your macOS version, what you said or did, and anything in `~/Library/Logs/Butterfly/crashes`.
